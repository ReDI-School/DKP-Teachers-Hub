# Teacher Trainings & Resources

At ReDI, we aim to support our volunteers with both teaching tools (platforms and materials) and training opportunities. We understand that being an expert doesn’t always mean knowing how to teach, so our trainings are designed around volunteers’ needs.

Most sessions are led pro-bono by experienced trainers. Some share materials, while others use their own resources, which makes active participation especially important to keep these collaborations going.

If you’re interested in becoming a trainer or know someone who is, contact: 📩 [tatiana@redi-school.org](mailto:tatiana@redi-school.org)

**Common topics include:**

* Session Design
* Feedback
* Managing different skill levels
* Diversity & inclusion
* Classroom leadership & facilitation
* Team & class management

### **Kids Program Toolkit: Practical Activities & Dynamics**

This practical toolkit was designed by Belén Clemente, a multilingual professional with a background in philology, language education, and instructional design. Drawing on her experience in curriculum development, digital learning, and multilingual adaptation, Belén brings a linguistic and intercultural perspective to creating learning experiences that are engaging, accessible, and responsive to diverse linguistic and cultural contexts.

This practical toolkit was designed by Belén Clemente, a multilingual professional with a background in philology, language education, and instructional design. It offers ReDI Kids volunteers four simple activities and strategies to support classroom management and create calm, focused, and engaging learning environments. The toolkit introduces basic pedagogical approaches that volunteers can try, adapt, and build on according to their group’s needs, helping them feel more confident and make their sessions smoother, more enjoyable, and rewarding for both themselves and the children.
